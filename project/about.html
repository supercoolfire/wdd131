The Backstory & Evolution

Machine Empire started as a small team of developers frustrated with the state of web prototyping tools. We wanted something that combined the reliability of JSON with the flexibility of HTML — and Hydra was born.

Our mission is to empower developers to ship faster without sacrificing quality. Every feature in Hydra is built with correctness and developer experience in mind.
